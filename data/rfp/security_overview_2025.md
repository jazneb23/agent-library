# Halcyon Data Security Overview
Last updated: 2025-08-02

## Encryption
Customer data is encrypted at rest with AES-256 and in transit with TLS 1.2 or higher.

## Data retention
Customer data is retained for 90 days after contract termination to allow for data export, then permanently deleted.

## Compliance
Our SOC 2 Type II audit is in progress. The report is expected in Q1 2027. Until it is issued, we cannot provide a SOC 2 report.

## Testing
A third party firm performs a penetration test every year. Summary results are available under NDA.

## Access control
Multi factor authentication (MFA) is available on all plans.
