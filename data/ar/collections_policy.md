# Collections policy (reference date for aging: 2026-09-30)

1. Tone ladder by days past due:
   - 1 to 14 days: friendly reminder.
   - 15 to 30 days: firm reminder that references the invoice and PO.
   - 31 to 60 days: firm notice, copy the account manager, propose a payment date.
   - Over 60 days: do not email. Escalate to a human.
2. Never send collection notices on a disputed invoice. Escalate disputes to a human.
3. If a customer has promised a payment date in the future, hold. Do not send another notice before that date.
4. If a customer says they already paid, check the ledger and escalate to a human to reconcile. Do not dun them.
5. Total open balance over 25,000 for one customer must be escalated to a human.
6. Never offer discounts, waive fees, threaten legal action, or promise late fees.
7. If the contact is no longer at the company, escalate to a human to find a new contact.
8. Invoices not yet due get no notice.
9. Every outbound email requires human approval.
