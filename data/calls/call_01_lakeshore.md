# Call 01: Lakeshore Logistics discovery
Date: 2026-09-08
Attendees: Sam (Halcyon SE), Priya Nair (VP Operations, Lakeshore), Marcus Chen (CFO, Lakeshore)

**Sam:** Priya, you mentioned weekly reporting is a pain. Can you walk me through it?
**Priya:** Every Monday three analysts spend two days pulling data from our warehouse and spreadsheets into the ops report. By the time leadership sees it, it is stale.
**Sam:** What would good look like?
**Priya:** Same day reporting. If we cut those two days to a few hours, that frees about six analyst days a week. That is the number I have taken to Marcus.
**Marcus:** And I have earmarked eighty thousand dollars for this in the first half of fiscal 27. Priya's case is solid.
**Sam:** Who else needs to weigh in?
**Priya:** IT security reviews any new vendor. They will want SSO and they want the data to stay in our warehouse. After that it comes to Marcus for signoff, and we aim to decide by November 15.
**Sam:** And on the contract side?
**Marcus:** Legal takes about three weeks for a new vendor agreement, so we should start that early.
**Sam:** Are you looking at any other tools?
**Priya:** Not really. We just live in spreadsheets today.
**Priya:** I will run the internal pilot and take the results to Marcus myself. I want this to work.
**Sam:** Great. I will send a pilot plan and a security packet this week.
